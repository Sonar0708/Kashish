Employee ID: ______________   Department: ______________

Tell the employee that answers are used to improve the workplace and are handled confidentially.

1. What is the main reason you decided to leave?
2. Was there a moment when you started considering other options? What happened?
3. How would you describe your relationship with your manager?
4. Did your role match what was described when you joined?
5. Did you have the tools, information and support to do your job well?
6. How did you feel about recognition and growth opportunities here?
7. What did you enjoy most about working here?
8. What one change would have made you stay?
9. Would you recommend this company to a friend? Why or why not?
10. Anything else you would like to share?

CODING GUIDE FOR SPOTTING TRENDS
After each interview, tag the main reason with ONE code and log it in your attrition tracker:
GROWTH · COMPENSATION · MANAGER · ROLE-MISMATCH · WORK-LIFE · RELOCATION · STUDIES · OTHER
Review the codes every quarter to see which reasons repeat.
